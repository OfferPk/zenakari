## Full Description (4,000 characters max)
Immerse yourself in ZenAkari, a serene and contemplative Japanese logic puzzle inspired by the traditional art of Akari (Light Up / 美術館).

Step into a quiet evening garden at twilight. Stone masonry walls and lantern posts wait in the gentle evening breeze. Your objective is simple yet deeply meditative: place traditional stone lanterns (石灯籠) along garden pathways so that every stone tile is illuminated by warm amber light, while honoring the sacred harmony of the garden.

=== THE RULES OF AKARI ===
1. Lantern Rays: Place lanterns on open path tiles. Each lantern casts rays of light orthogonally in four directions (North, South, East, West) across all unobstructed pathway tiles until reaching a stone wall or the edge of the garden.
2. No Cross-Illumination: No two lanterns may illuminate one another. There cannot be an unobstructed straight line of sight between any two lanterns.
3. Numbered Stone Walls: Stone walls etched with a number (0, 1, 2, 3, or 4) specify exactly how many lanterns must touch them orthogonally. Solid black walls without numbers impose no adjacent restrictions.
4. Total Illumination: Every pathway stone in the garden must receive light from at least one lantern.
