Art of Akari (Light Up)

In the quiet evening garden, place traditional stone lanterns (石灯籠) along open pathways so every stone is illuminated by gentle warm rays.

Illumination

Light all pathways

1. Radiant Lantern Rays

Each lantern casts light straight horizontally and vertically in all 4 directions across pathway cells until hitting a stone wall or garden edge.

2. No Direct Sightline

No two lanterns may illuminate one another. There cannot be an unblocked straight line of sight between any two lanterns.

3. Numbered Stone Walls

A numbered stone wall indicates exactly how many lanterns must touch it orthogonally (top, bottom, left, right). Unnumbered black walls have no restriction.

4. Touch Controls

Tap any pathway tile to place a Lantern. Tap again for a Dot marker (to note a cell cannot have a lantern), or use the Lantern/Dot toggle button below.
